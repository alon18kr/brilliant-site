Brilliant brand kit.

How to write and show the Brilliant name, logo and colours. Please do not alter the logo or set it in another typeface.

Name

Always "Brilliant", with a capital B and a full stop in the logo only. Never "Brilliant App".

Colours

Brand blue, soft grey background, near-black text. Use the brand blue for one action per screen.

Logo

Wordmark only, minimum height 24 px, clear space equal to the height of the B on all sides.

Voice

Plain sentences, present tense, no exclamation marks. We describe what the product does, not how excited we are.
